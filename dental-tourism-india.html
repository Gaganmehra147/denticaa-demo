Help & FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions on Dental Tourism

Q. Why is dental treatment so affordable in India without compromising quality?

Dental education, clinic infrastructure, and operational overheads in India are significantly lower than in the USA, UK, or Australia. At Denticaa, we use the exact same FDA-approved global implant systems (Straumann, Nobel Biocare) and Swiss/German materials, delivering world-class results at 70-80% lower cost.

Q. How do I travel to Jabalpur, India?

Jabalpur Airport (JLR) has multiple daily direct flights from major international hubs like New Delhi (DEL), Mumbai (BOM), Hyderabad (HYD), and Bengaluru (BLR). Our clinic is located in upscale Wright Town, just 20 minutes from the airport.

Q. Can I communicate easily in English?

Yes, 100%! Dr. Kapil Jain, Dr. Mrs Anmoll Jain, and our international coordinator team are completely fluent in English. We ensure clear, transparent, and comfortable communication at every stage.

Q. Do you provide warranty on implants and crowns?

Yes. All dental implants and CAD/CAM zirconia crowns come with international manufacturer warranty certificates and authentic QR-tracked authenticity cards.
