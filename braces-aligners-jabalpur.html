Braces vs Clear Aligners

Understanding your orthodontic alignment options at Denticaa.

Traditional & Ceramic Braces

High-precision orthodontic brackets (metal or tooth-colored ceramic) attached to teeth to correct severe crowding, rotation, and complex bite issues with reliable long-term stability.

Clear Aligners (Invisible Braces)

Removable, transparent custom trays designed to gently shift teeth into position. Virtually invisible, easy to clean, and requires zero dietary restrictions.
