Root Canal Treatment in Jabalpur

Root Canal Treatment (RCT) is performed to relieve severe tooth pain and preserve damaged or infected natural teeth by cleaning and sealing the interior root canals under local anesthesia.

When Is RCT Required?

Persistent tooth pain, sensitivity to hot/cold, swelling around gums, or deep decay affecting the tooth nerve pulp.

Patient Comfort First

At Denticaa, we use modern endodontic rotary techniques and local anesthesia to ensure your root canal treatment is comfortable and effective.
